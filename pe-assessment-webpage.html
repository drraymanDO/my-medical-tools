Pulmonary Embolism Assessment

Step-by-step tool based on the 2026 AHA/ACC/Multisociety Acute PE Guideline

This tool is a clinical documentation and reasoning aid based on the 2026 AHA/ACC/ACCP/ACEP/CHEST/SCAI/SHM/SIR/SVM/SVN Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Acute Pulmonary Embolism in Adults. It does not replace clinical judgment, the full guideline text, or local institutional protocols. Not for use as the sole basis for a treatment decision.
